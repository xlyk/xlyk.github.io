Welcome to this modern dark-themed blog! This design philosophy focuses on creating a reading experience that's easy on the eyes while maintaining a sophisticated, contemporary aesthetic.

## Why Dark Mode Matters

Dark mode isn't just a trend—it's a thoughtful design choice that offers several benefits:

1. **Reduced eye strain** during extended reading sessions
2. **Better battery life** on OLED screens
3. **Improved focus** by reducing visual distractions
4. **Aesthetic appeal** that feels modern and clean

## Design Principles

Our dark theme follows these key principles:

### Color Psychology
We use warm dark backgrounds instead of pure black, creating a softer viewing experience. The soft lavender text provides excellent readability without the harshness of pure white.

### Thoughtful Accents
The color palette includes:
- **Soft blue** for links and primary interactions
- **Gentle purple** for secondary accents
- **Warm amber** for highlights
- **Muted green** for success states
